Privacy Policy.

Last updated: July 27, 2026

This website, gregoryshepard.com ("the Site"), is the personal website of Gregory Shepard. Your privacy matters, and this page explains plainly what the Site does and does not do with information about you.

What this Site collects

The Site is a static informational website. It does not require accounts and does not currently run advertising or behavioral analytics on the new site. When you choose to send the contact form, the information you enter is processed so Gregory's team can receive and answer your inquiry.

  • Hosting logs. The Site is served by a web hosting provider, which may automatically log standard technical data such as your IP address, browser type, and pages requested. That data is handled under the host's own privacy policy.
  • Contact form. If you send the form, we receive your name, email address, selected topic, message, and the page that referred you to the form. The form is delivered by FormSubmit, which states that form submissions are retained for 30 days. FormSubmit may use reCAPTCHA for spam screening.
  • Email. If you contact us directly by email, we receive whatever you choose to send and use it to respond to you and manage the relationship you requested.

Third-party services we link to

The Site uses FormSubmit to deliver contact-form messages and links to external services including Eventbrite (event registration), WeFunder (investment campaign), StartupScience.io (founder platform), HubSpot (meeting scheduling), and social platforms such as LinkedIn, YouTube, X, and Spotify. Information handled by those services is governed by their own privacy policies. We encourage you to read them.

What we don't do

  • We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information.
  • We do not run advertising networks on the Site.
  • We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Your choices

The Site does not maintain visitor accounts or profiles. If you have sent a form or email and would like the correspondence corrected or deleted, contact us and identify the message. We will address the request subject to any legal or operational retention requirements.

Changes

If the Site later adds analytics, advertising pixels, a newsletter database, or shop checkout, this policy will be updated before those features are activated, with a new "last updated" date above.

Questions about this policy: greg@startupscience.io
