The Deep Dive

The 2-Day, with room to go all the way.

Same lifecycle. More time. More depth.

The 2-Day Intensive moves fast: seven phases, a portfolio of real artifacts by the end of day two. The 5-Day Deep Dive takes the same lifecycle and gives every phase the time it deserves: deeper work on vision and product, more hands-on go-to-market and standardization, and dedicated time to apply each step directly to your own company, with feedback in the room.

If the 2-Day is the sprint that proves the system, the 5-Day is the build: the same framework, slowed down enough that you leave with a company measurably further along the lifecycle than when you walked in.
